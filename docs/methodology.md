# Renewable Energy Analysis Methodology

## Core measures
- Installed capacity: preserve MW/GW units and distinguish nameplate from operational capacity.
- Electricity generation: report MWh/GWh with the reporting interval.
- Capacity factor: actual generation / theoretical generation at rated capacity for the same period.
- Renewable share: renewable generation / total generation for the same geography and period.
- Emissions intensity: emissions / electricity generated, only where source methodology is comparable.

## Validation
- Standardise units and time zones.
- Confirm whether values are capacity, generation, or consumption.
- Check duplicate plant and reporting-period records.
- Flag negative generation and impossible capacity values.
- Record missing intervals and estimated observations.
- Preserve source, licence, geography, and update date.

## Questions
1. How does generation mix change over time?
2. Which technologies contribute most installed capacity?
3. How does capacity factor vary by technology?
4. Are seasonal patterns visible in generation?

## Caveats
Weather, curtailment, outages, and data coverage affect output. Do not compare emissions factors without checking their boundaries.
